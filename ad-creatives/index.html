Research Key Findings

What's Working Right Now

  • Short-form video (14-15 sec) generates 403% more enquiries than static images

Australian Cost of Living — Your Emotional Fuel

  • Real disposable income collapsed 8% — largest decline in recorded history
  • 10.6 years to save a house deposit. Rent up 59.8% over decade
  • 3.3 million Australians in poverty. 36% named cost of living as #1 election issue
  • Mining described as "the only group able to buy a typical house"

March 31 Regulation — Genuine Urgency

  • All Bali short-term rentals must have SBG + SLF to stay on Airbnb/Booking.com
  • Only 25% of Bali villas comply — our villas are fully licensed
  • Non-compliant villas face fines, forced closure, permanent delisting

Key Rules

  • 14-16 seconds is the sweet spot across Meta platforms — all 3 videos hit this
  • No logo or brand intro — hook first, always
  • Bold text overlay from first frame — 85% of FB video is watched muted
  • Rotate creatives every 7 days (37% performance drop after)
  • Kill underperformers after 3 days / $50 spend with no engagement
  • Scale winners by 20% daily max
  • Upload all 3 formats per ad — Meta auto-selects best format per placement

Next Steps — UGC Content

These AI-generated videos are strong for launch. For maximum conversion, film a real Australian walking through the villa, talking to camera: "I can't believe this is what $170K gets you in Bali." UGC delivers 4x higher CTR and 50% lower CPC than polished content.
