# Real Estate & Property Investment Advertising Report
## Targeting Australians — Facebook, Instagram, YouTube (2025-2026)

*Compiled March 2026 for North Canggu Villas Campaign*

---

## 1. TOP 5 AD FORMATS WORKING RIGHT NOW

### #1: Short-Form Video (15-30 seconds)
Properties marketed with video receive **403% more enquiries**. Short video walkthroughs with captions boost engagement by 42% vs static. On Meta, **14 seconds** is the sweet spot.

### #2: UGC-Style Video Ads
**4x higher CTR** and **50% lower CPC** than polished/branded ads. Raw, single-take, minimal-edit content outperforms studio quality. Bypasses the "ad filter" viewers have developed.

### #3: Carousel Ads
Multi-image carousels drive **3x more clicks** than single-property ads. Especially effective for price comparisons and lifestyle contrasts.

### #4: Video Testimonials / Social Proof
Testimonial ads increase conversion rates by **270%** for real estate. Video testimonials from actual buyers carry authentic credibility.

### #5: Lead Ads (In-Platform Forms)
Generate leads at **$16-$50 AUD** per lead. "Learn More" and "Schedule Now" CTAs outperform "Contact Us" significantly.

## 3. VIDEO VS STATIC

**Video wins decisively:**
- 403% more enquiries than non-video
- 2.2x more engagement than static images
- Instagram algorithm favours Reels over static
- Instagram Reels: 4.72% CTR | Facebook Feed: 5.51% CTR

**When static still works:**
- Carousel ads for price comparisons
- Retargeting warm audiences
- Testimonials with bold text overlays
- Complementary assets in mixed campaigns

**Rule:** Lead with video for cold audiences. Static for retargeting and carousels for mid-funnel.

## 5. WINNING FIRST 3 SECONDS

Users see 1,500+ ads daily. Scroll speed is 41% faster than 2020. You have 0.5-1.7 seconds.

**Must include:**
1. Bold text overlay or subtitle (many watch without sound)
2. Movement or visual disruption (not a static establishing shot)
3. No logo, no brand intro, no name — hook first
4. A specific number or claim ("$247K for THIS in Bali")
5. A face talking directly to camera (for UGC)

**Hook formulas that work:**
- "What [$X] gets you in [Location A] vs [Location B]"
- "I just bought a villa in Bali for less than a Sydney deposit"
- "The biggest mistake Australians make when investing overseas"
- "This changes everything for Bali property investors"
- "POV: You wake up in your Bali investment villa"

## 6. UGC VS POLISHED

### UGC Performance:
- **4x higher CTR** than branded ads
- **50% lower CPC**
- 31% more memorable
- 28% feel more unique to customers

### When Polished Works:
- Luxury/aspirational positioning (retargeting)
- Brand-building campaigns
- Hero content for YouTube pre-roll

### Recommendation:
**70% UGC for cold acquisition. 30% polished for retargeting and brand.**

## 11. CAMPAIGN STRUCTURE

### Phase 1: Cold Traffic (Weeks 1-2)
- **Platform:** Facebook Feed + Instagram Reels (80% of spend)
- **Creative:** UGC-style video + static (3-5 hook variations)
- **Budget:** $80-150/day
- **Rotate creatives every 7 days** (37% performance drop after 7 days)

### Phase 2: Warm Retargeting (Weeks 2-4)
- **Platform:** Facebook + Instagram + YouTube
- **Creative:** Carousels, testimonials, polished villa footage
- **Budget:** $40-60/day
- **Target:** Video viewers (50%+), website visitors

### Phase 3: Hot Conversion (Week 3+)
- **Platform:** Facebook + Instagram lead ads
- **Creative:** Urgency (March 31), scarcity, limited availability
- **Budget:** $30-50/day

### Testing:
- Test 3-5 hook variations per concept
- Kill underperformers after 3 days / $50 spend
- Scale winners by 20% daily max
- A/B test UGC vs polished for each concept
